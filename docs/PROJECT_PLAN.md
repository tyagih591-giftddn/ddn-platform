# DDN – District Delivery Network

## Brand
DDN – District Delivery Network

## Version 1 Scope
Delivery booking only.

## Launch Area
Indirapuram, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh.

## Core Flow
1. Customer creates a delivery booking.
2. Backend stores the booking.
3. Admin reviews and assigns the delivery.
4. Rider receives the delivery task.
5. Delivery status is updated.

## Future Expansion
- More areas and districts
- Customer accounts
- Rider onboarding
- Pricing engine
- Payments
- Live tracking
